Business context

Why the Ads Agent Was Built

As campaign volume, historical data and budget complexity increased, repeated manual analysis became fragmented and time-consuming. The Ads Budget Intelligence Agent was built to turn that workflow into forecasting, scenario simulation, budget optimisation, risk/opportunity intelligence and explainable recommendations.
