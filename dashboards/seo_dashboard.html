Interpretation

From SEO Execution to AI Decision Intelligence

2023–2025 expanded the ranking footprint through technical SEO, category/product optimisation, content planning, backlink/PR and structured measurement. In 2026, the focus shifted toward ranking quality, CTR efficiency, GEO visibility and AI-assisted decision support.

GA4 organic sessions and Search Console clicks are different metrics. Revenue is influenced by pricing, campaigns, paid media and other commercial factors, so it should not be attributed to SEO alone.
Data scope: 2023–2025 historical SEO/Power BI reports, 2026 Jan–Aug GA4/Search Console reports and 2025/2026 Search Console comparison workbook. Historical portfolio dashboard; live private production data and credentials are not exposed.
